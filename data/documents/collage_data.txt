Welcome to ABC Engineering College, Hyderabad. Admission Rules and Guidelines for 2026-2027.

1. Courses Offered and Seat Capacity:
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): 180 seats
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE): 120 seats
- Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE): 60 seats
- Mechanical Engineering (ME): 60 seats

2. Fee Structure (Per Year):
- Tuition Fee for CSE and ECE: INR 1,20,000 per year.
- Tuition Fee for EEE and Mechanical: INR 90,000 per year.
- Hostel and Mess Fee: INR 75,000 per year (Separate hostels for boys and girls with 24/7 Wi-Fi and security).
- Caution Deposit (Refundable): INR 10,000 one-time.

3. Admission Eligibility and Cutoffs:
- Students must clear Class 12 / Intermediate with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) with a minimum of 60% marks.
- CSE Admission Criteria: Merit-based. Usually requires a minimum of 85% or above in Intermediate exams.
- ECE and EEE Criteria: Requires a minimum of 70% or above in Intermediate exams.
- Management Quota: Available for students with less than 70% marks, subject to seat availability and higher fee structures.

4. Placements and Recruiters:
- Highest Package offered last year: INR 45 Lakhs Per Annum (LPA) by Microsoft.
- Average Package: INR 6.5 Lakhs Per Annum (LPA).
- Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant, Amazon, and Microsoft.

5. Scholarships:
- Merit Scholarship: 50% tuition fee waiver for students scoring above 95% in Class 12.
- Sports Scholarship: 25% tuition fee waiver for National/State level sports certificate holders.
